A/C troubleshooting

What to do when your A/C stops cooling

A few observations can make your service call more useful.

Read the guide

Notice whether the system is running, whether air is coming from the vents, and whether it feels warmer than usual. If you hear an unusual sound or notice a burning smell, turn the system off and explain what happened when you call.

Tell the technician when the issue started and whether it affects the whole house. If the system isn't producing airflow or is blowing hot air, contact Polar Express to discuss the next step.

Seasonal care

Why schedule an A/C or heating checkup?

A checkup is a chance to catch wear and ask how your system is performing.

Read the guide

Cooling and heating equipment work hard at different times of year. A scheduled checkup gives a technician time to inspect the system, discuss any concerns, and recommend maintenance if it is needed.

Polar Express offers seasonal A/C and heating checkups. Ask what is included and when a visit makes sense for your equipment.

Repair or replace

Questions to ask before replacing a system

Understand the diagnosis before you make a bigger decision.

Read the guide

Ask what failed, what a repair would involve, and whether the technician expects the repair to address the underlying issue. If replacement is recommended, ask about the options and get a written estimate.

Polar Express says repair is its first option. A conversation about both paths can help you choose what fits your home.
