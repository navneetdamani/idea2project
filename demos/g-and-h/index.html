Comfort notes

Good questions to ask.

Simple starting points for a more informed conversation with an HVAC technician.

01 · Repairs

Repair or replace the A/C?

Start with a diagnosis. Ask what failed, whether a repair is practical, and how the system's age and condition affect the decision. G&H says it looks for a sensible repair before recommending replacement.

02 · Maintenance

When should I schedule a tune-up?

A cooling check ahead of warm weather and a heating check before cooler months can help you prepare. Ask G&H what is covered by its maintenance agreement and what your system needs.

03 · Comfort

Why is one room warmer?

Airflow, insulation, sun exposure and equipment can all play a part. Make a note of when the room feels different, then describe the pattern when you call so the technician has a useful starting point.
